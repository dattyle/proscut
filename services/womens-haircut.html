Cuts & styling

Cuts that grow out as nicely as they go in

The best haircut isn't just the one that looks great walking out — it's the one that still looks good two months later. Our Sunnyvale stylists take the time to listen first. We'll ask how you actually wear your hair, what you don't have time to do, and what you'd like to stop fighting with.

What to expect

Your appointment, start to finish

Most women's haircuts at our Sunnyvale salon take 45 to 75 minutes. Here's roughly how that breaks down:

  • 1. Consultation — we listen first, talk about length, layers, and lifestyle
  • 2. Wash & condition — at the chair, with a moment to actually relax
  • 3. Cut — wet or dry, depending on what works best for your hair
  • 4. Style & finish — quick blowout to show you how the cut sits
  • 5. Mirror check — front, sides, back, before you leave

Between visits

Keeping your cut looking fresh

A few simple habits stretch the life of a great haircut:

  • Trim every 6 to 10 weeks for most styles
  • Stop in for a quick bang trim any time — it's a five-minute reset
  • Use a heat protectant if you blow-dry or iron daily
  • Air-dry when you can — your hair will thank you
  • Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce frizz

Common questions

Women's haircut FAQ

How long does a women's haircut take?

A women's haircut at our Sunnyvale salon usually runs 45 to 75 minutes, depending on hair length and whether you're getting a blowout. We build in time for the consultation so the cut actually fits how you wear your hair.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome any day we're open. For a blowout or updo we suggest calling ahead so we can set aside enough time. Reach us at (408) 774-1917.

Should I bring a photo of the haircut I want?

Yes — photos help a lot, even a screenshot. Bring two or three if you can: one of the length, one of the layers or texture. It saves us both the back-and-forth of guessing what you mean.

How often should I get my hair trimmed?

For most styles, every 6 to 10 weeks keeps the shape and the ends looking healthy. If you're growing it out, you can stretch trims a little longer — your stylist will tell you when it needs it.

Should I wash my hair before my appointment?

Either way is fine. We wash you at the chair as part of the service, so don't go out of your way. If you're getting just a quick trim, a clean dry head is easiest.

Can you do an updo for a wedding or special occasion?

Yes. Call ahead so we can hold time for it. For weddings, a quick trial run a couple of weeks before the event is a good idea.
