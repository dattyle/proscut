What to expect

Your color appointment, start to finish

Color appointments at our Sunnyvale salon usually run 90 minutes to three hours. Roughly how the time breaks down:

  • 1. Consultation — we look at your hair, talk through tone and dimension
  • 2. Sectioning & application — color or foils, applied with care
  • 3. Process time — chemistry does its job; bring a book or scroll your phone
  • 4. Rinse & tone — locks in the color and balances the tone
  • 5. Style & finish — quick blowout to show you the final result

Touch-up timing

How often to come back

The right cadence depends on what you got and how visible your roots are:

  • Gray coverage — every 4 to 6 weeks
  • Single-process — every 6 to 8 weeks
  • Partial highlights — every 8 to 12 weeks
  • Full highlights — every 10 to 16 weeks
  • Quick root touch-up — anytime in between

If you've stretched between visits longer than you meant to, no problem. We'll get you sorted.

Caring for your color

Aftercare that keeps your color looking fresh

A few habits make a real difference in how long your color holds its tone:

  • Wait 48 hours before your first wash
  • Use a sulfate-free, color-safe shampoo
  • Wash less often — every other day at most for color-treated hair
  • Rinse with cool water — heat strips color faster
  • Use a heat protectant before flat irons or blow-dryers
  • Ask us about gloss treatments to refresh tone between appointments

Common questions

Hair coloring FAQ

How long does a hair color appointment take?

Plan on roughly 90 minutes to three hours, depending on the service. A root touch-up usually runs 90 minutes; full highlights or a major color change can run two to three hours. Long, thick hair takes the upper end of those ranges.

How often should I touch up my color or roots?

For gray coverage, every four to six weeks keeps the line invisible. For highlights, six to eight weeks is more typical. We'll give you a realistic timeline based on your hair growth and how visible the line tends to be.

Will hair color damage my hair?

Color is a chemistry service, so the condition of your hair matters. We'll take a look first and pick a process — and a strength — that fits what your hair can handle. Most clients leave with hair that feels healthy and looks fresh.

Can I get a haircut and color the same day?

Yes — and most of the time it's actually easier to do them together. Call ahead so we can block enough time. The cut typically goes after the color so we shape around the new tone.

Should I wash my hair before a color appointment?

Come in with hair that's clean but not freshly washed the same morning. A day-old wash gives your scalp's natural oils a chance to protect against irritation. Skip heavy oils, masks, or dry shampoo on the day.

Do I need to bring a photo for the color I want?

Yes — photos help a lot. Bring two or three if you can: one of the overall tone, one of the dimension or highlights pattern. Lighting in photos is tricky, so we'll talk through what's realistic for your starting point.
