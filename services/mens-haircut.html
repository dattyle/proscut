What to expect

Your appointment, start to finish

A typical men's haircut at our Sunnyvale salon runs 30 to 45 minutes; cut + beard usually closer to an hour.

  • 1. Quick consult — we ask how you wear it and what you want changed
  • 2. Wash & section — clean hair makes for cleaner lines
  • 3. Cut — clipper and scissor work, your call on length
  • 4. Beard / detail — if you're getting it, this is where it happens
  • 5. Style & check — quick blow-dry and a mirror check before you leave

Between visits

How often to come back

Different cuts hold their shape for different amounts of time:

  • Skin / high fade — every 2 to 3 weeks for sharp lines
  • Mid / low fade or taper — every 3 to 5 weeks
  • Classic / scissor cut — every 5 to 8 weeks
  • Buzz cut — whenever it bothers you, usually 2 to 4 weeks

If you forget and let it go, that's fine too. We'll get you back on track.

Common questions

Men's haircut FAQ

How long does a men's haircut in Sunnyvale take?

Most men's haircuts take 30 to 45 minutes. A cut plus a beard trim usually runs closer to an hour. We don't rush the clipper work.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome any day we're open. If you're tight on time, give us a call at (408) 774-1917 first and we'll let you know the wait.

Do you do beard trims and cut + beard combos?

Yes. We do clean-line beard trims, edge-ups, and full cut-and-beard combos. Most guys book the combo — easier than two separate trips.

How often should I get a men's haircut?

Fades and tapers usually look sharpest if you come in every three to five weeks. Classic and longer styles can stretch to six or eight. We'll tell you what fits your hair and your routine.

Do you offer senior haircuts?

Yes, we have a senior haircut option. Stop in or call (408) 774-1917 with any questions about it.

Should I bring a photo?

Photos help a lot — even a screenshot from your phone gets us on the same page faster than words alone. No pressure though, we're happy to talk through what you want.
