Permanent waves & curls

Curls and waves you'll actually wear

A perm is one of the easiest ways to give your hair lasting shape — soft beach waves on flat hair, body and bounce on fine hair, or a head full of real curls. At Pros Cut on East El Camino Real, we've been doing hair perming in Sunnyvale for over thirty years. Every perm starts with a real conversation about what you're picturing and what your hair can comfortably do.

If you've never had a perm before, or if it's been a while, that's the most common kind of client we see. We'll walk through the options with you, check the condition of your hair, and help you pick a style that's going to feel like you — not like a poodle, not like a 1980s prom photo.

Choices

The four perms we do most

We keep our perm menu simple and grounded in what actually looks good day-to-day in Sunnyvale weather. No gimmicks.

  • Beach waves — loose, lived-in texture
  • Body waves — soft, even movement and volume
  • Spiral curls — defined ringlet curls
  • Classic tight perms — full, springy curls that hold

Not sure which one is for you? That's what the consultation is for. We'll take a look at your hair and tell you the truth about what's likely to work.

What to expect

Your perm appointment, start to finish

Most perm appointments at our Sunnyvale salon run about two to three hours. Here's roughly how the time breaks down:

  • 1. Consultation — we look at your hair and talk through the look
  • 2. Wash & section — clean hair, divided into manageable sections
  • 3. Wrap — your stylist wraps each section onto rods sized to the curl you want
  • 4. Solution & process — perming solution goes on; we let chemistry do the work
  • 5. Rinse & neutralize — locks in the new shape
  • 6. Style & send-off — a quick blowout so you walk out feeling great

Bring something to read or watch — there's no rush, and we don't cut corners on processing time.

Picking your perm

How to choose the right perm for your hair

The perm that looks great on your friend may not be the right one for your hair, and that's not a bad thing. Three things we look at first:

  • Hair length — long hair holds looser waves beautifully; short hair shines with tighter curls
  • Hair texture — fine hair benefits from body waves; thick hair can carry spiral or classic curls
  • Daily routine — how much time you actually want to spend on styling tomorrow

If your morning routine is wash-and-go, we'll steer you toward a perm that doesn't need irons. If you enjoy styling, we have more room to play.

Caring for your perm

Aftercare that keeps your perm looking fresh

A few simple habits stretch the life of a perm by months:

  • Wait 48 hours before your first wash
  • Use a sulfate-free shampoo and a moisturizing conditioner
  • Skip the high-heat tools when you can — air-dry or use a diffuser
  • Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce frizz
  • Get regular trims to keep the ends from looking tired

Your stylist will go over the specifics for your hair before you leave. We'd rather over-explain than have you guessing at home.

Common questions

Hair perming FAQ

How long does a hair perm last?

Most perms hold their shape for three to six months, depending on your hair type, how often you wash it, and how you style it day-to-day. Looser styles like beach waves tend to relax sooner; tighter perms hold longer.

Will a perm damage my hair?

A perm is a chemistry service, so we always do a quick consultation first to make sure your hair is in good shape for it. We use conditioning solutions and pace the process to your hair type — most clients leave with hair that feels healthy and looks fresh.

How long does a perm appointment take?

Plan on roughly two to three hours at our Sunnyvale salon. Long, thick hair takes the upper end of that range; short hair is usually faster. Call (408) 774-1917 to check the wait.

Can I get a perm if my hair is colored?

Yes, in many cases — but timing matters. We'll look at the condition of your color-treated hair and may recommend waiting a couple of weeks between coloring and perming, or starting with a deep conditioning treatment. Stop in and we'll take a look.

Should I wash my hair before a perm appointment?

Come in with hair that's clean but not freshly washed the same morning. A day-old wash gives the perming solution something to work with. Skip heavy product, oils, or dry shampoo on the day of your appointment.

What's the difference between beach waves and a body wave perm?

Beach waves are looser, more relaxed, and meant to look effortless — think days-after-the-ocean texture. A body wave adds soft, even movement throughout, giving fine or limp hair more shape and volume without obvious curls.
