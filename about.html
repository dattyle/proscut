Our story

A familiar chair on El Camino

For over twenty years, the same friendly hands and the same fair prices.

Pros Cut, since 2002

Pros Cut sits in a small storefront on East El Camino Real, right at the corner of Henderson Avenue in Sunnyvale, California. We're not flashy and we don't try to be — we're the place locals send friends to when they want a real cut at a real price.

Our lead stylist Mary has been cutting hair here for over two decades. Her clients include kids whose first haircuts she gave who now bring in their own kids. That kind of trust isn't something you build overnight.

Whether you walk in for a quick fade, a color refresh, a perm, or a manicure, you'll get the same thing every time: skilled hands, honest prices, and a welcome that makes you want to come back. We serve Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Cupertino, Santa Clara, and the surrounding South Bay.
