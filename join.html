Rent a salon station in Sunnyvale

Hair stylists and barbers looking for a chair in Sunnyvale — join an established salon with steady walk-in traffic and a loyal neighborhood clientele.

Your chair, our shop

Pros Cut has been on East El Camino Real in Sunnyvale since 1989. We're a neighborhood salon — not a franchise — and we welcome licensed hair stylists and barbers who want to rent a station and build their own book of clients under one roof.

Whether you specialize in fades, women's cuts, color, perms, or a mix of services, you keep your clients and your schedule. We handle the storefront, the reputation, and the foot traffic that comes with decades on this corner.

Booth rental details — availability, terms, and what's included — vary. The best way to find out if a station is open and whether Pros Cut is the right fit is a quick phone call.
