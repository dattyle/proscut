Caring for your nails

Tips that keep your polish looking fresh

A few simple habits stretch the life of a manicure or pedicure:

  • Wear gloves for cleaning, dishes, and gardening
  • Apply a clear top coat every few days for shine
  • Use cuticle oil daily — keeps nail beds healthy
  • Avoid acetone-heavy removers; opt for gentle ones
  • For pedicures, give polish a few extra minutes to set before slipping on shoes

After waxing, skip sun, hot showers, and heavy makeup for 24 hours. The skin needs a beat to settle.

Common questions

Manicure & pedicure FAQ

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome any day we're open. For a mani-pedi combo or if you want a specific time, call ahead — we'll let you know the wait. Reach us at (408) 774-1917.

How long does a manicure or pedicure take?

A classic manicure runs about 30 to 45 minutes. A pedicure is closer to 45 to 60 minutes. A mani-pedi combo is typically 75 to 90 minutes total. We don't rush the soak or the polish.

Are your tools sterilized between clients?

Yes. We sanitize every metal tool between clients and use fresh files and buffers. The pedicure basin is cleaned and sanitized between every appointment.

Do you offer gel polish?

Yes — gel polish is available for both manicures and pedicures. It typically lasts two to three weeks before you'll want a refresh. Ask us at the chair if you'd like gel or regular polish.

Can I bring my own polish?

Of course — bring your own bottle if there's a specific shade you love and want to use again. We've got plenty of in-house options too if you'd rather pick from what's there.

Can I add eyebrow or lip waxing?

Yes. Brow shaping, lip, and chin waxing are easy add-ons while you're here. Mention it when you check in and we'll fold it into your appointment time.
