First haircuts, last-minute haircuts, every haircut in between

We've been doing kids' haircuts in Sunnyvale for over thirty years, and we know that no two kids — or two haircuts — are the same. Some kids sit perfectly still and want to chat the whole time. Some need a snack and a tablet. Some cry. Some don't. We've seen it all, and we don't get rattled.

What we do every time: we kneel down, say hi, give them a second to look around, and only then start. No rushing, no scary clipper noises right out of the gate. By the time we're done, most kids are asking when they can come back.

First haircuts

What to expect for a first haircut

First haircuts can go a hundred different ways. A few things we've learned over the years:

  • Bring a snack — distraction is your friend
  • A favorite toy or screen helps — no judgment from us
  • Sit them on your lap if they want — totally fine
  • We'll go fast when needed — and slow when it helps
  • Ask for the keepsake curl — we'll set one aside for the baby book

If your child gets overwhelmed, that's OK too. We can pause, regroup, or finish another day. There's no rush.

When to come

Best times for a stress-free kids' haircut

Some times of the day are easier than others, especially for younger kids:

  • Mid-afternoon weekdays — usually shortest wait

Common questions

Kids' haircut FAQ

What's the best age for a first haircut?

Whenever they have enough hair to cut and you're ready. We've done plenty of first haircuts for one-year-olds and plenty for three-year-olds. There's no wrong age. Bring a snack, a phone with a favorite show, or whatever else helps.

My kid hates getting their hair cut. Can you handle that?

We've seen everything — wiggly toddlers, nervous five-year-olds, the kid who only sits still for tablets. We're patient, we work fast when we need to, and we know when to take a break. No judgment if it takes a couple of tries.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome any day we're open. Mid-afternoon weekdays usually have the shortest wait. Call (408) 774-1917 if you want to check the line before you head over.

How long does a kids' haircut take?

Most kids' haircuts take 15 to 30 minutes. First haircuts and especially nervous kids can take a little longer — we don't rush them.

Can you save a curl from my child's first haircut?

Yes — let us know at the start and we'll set aside a snip for you to take home. Lots of parents want a keepsake for the baby book.

Do you do after-school appointments?

We're open until 7 PM Monday through Saturday and until 4 PM on Sundays — plenty of time after school. Walk in any day, no appointment needed.
